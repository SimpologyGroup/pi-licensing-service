How It Works

  1. Choose a plan and pay by card through Paddle's secure checkout.
  2. We email your licence to the address you gave, within a few minutes.
  3. In the app, an administrator opens Subscription, clicks Find Licences and chooses Use This Licence.

One licence is for one account. The subscription renews every year until you cancel it; after a cancellation it runs to the end of the paid year and the account then moves back to the free tier.
